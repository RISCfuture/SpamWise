Connecting with IMAP

SpamWise usually works through Apple Mail. It can instead connect directly to one IMAP account over a secure connection.

  1. In Settings → Accounts, under Mail connection, set Connection to IMAP, then choose Switch to IMAP. Dry run turns on.
  2. Under IMAP account, enter the Server, your Username and your Password. If your provider uses another port, open Connection options to change the TLS port.
  3. Choose Save Connection. The password is saved in Keychain.
  4. Choose Check Connection. SpamWise signs in, finds the Inbox and Junk mailboxes and reads mail without changing it.
  5. When you’re ready, turn off dry run in Settings → General.

The server must support moving messages and identify its Junk mailbox; otherwise SpamWise leaves mail unchanged. If you move a message SpamWise put in Junk back to Inbox with another mail app, SpamWise treats it as marked Good.

To stop using IMAP, choose Forget Connection…. SpamWise removes the password from Keychain and switches back to Apple Mail with dry run on. Mail on the server stays as it is.
