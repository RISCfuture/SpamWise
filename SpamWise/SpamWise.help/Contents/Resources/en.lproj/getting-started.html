Getting started

SpamWise sorts your incoming mail in Apple Mail. Good mail stays in Inbox. Review mail stays in Inbox with an orange flag or background, so you can check it. Junk moves to the account’s Junk mailbox. SpamWise never deletes mail or moves it to Trash.

Set up SpamWise

When you first open SpamWise, it walks you through four steps.

  1. Read how SpamWise handles your privacy, then choose Continue.
  2. Choose a Provider, which classifies the mail your rules and known senders don’t decide:
    • Apple on-device classifies mail on this Mac with Apple Intelligence. Mail isn’t sent anywhere, and there’s no API key or charge.
    • Claude, OpenAI and Gemini classify mail with your own API key, and your provider account is charged for each message. Enter the key, choose Verify and save key or Save key, then turn on “Allow mail context to be sent to provider”.
    To decide later, choose Skip for now; you can choose a provider any time in Settings → Classifier.
  3. Choose Check Mail Connection and allow SpamWise to control Mail when macOS asks. SpamWise finds each account’s Inbox and Junk mailboxes without changing any mail. To help it recognize people you know, choose Connect Contacts and Import Inbox and Sent History.
  4. Choose Dry run recent mail to see what SpamWise would do with your 20 newest Inbox messages. Nothing in Mail changes. Then choose Finish setup.

To go through these steps again, choose Show setup in Settings → General.

Start filtering

Dry run stays on after setup: Activity shows what SpamWise would do, and your mail stays where it is. When the decisions look right:

  1. Choose SpamWise → Settings, then General.
  2. Turn off “Dry run — do not change mail”, then choose Turn off dry run.

The switch becomes available once SpamWise has found the Inbox and Junk mailboxes of at least one account, and only those accounts are filtered. SpamWise checks every move afterward. If Mail doesn’t confirm a move, SpamWise doesn’t retry or reverse it, and Activity shows the failure so you can check the message in Mail.

While dry run stays on, SpamWise checks each message once, even if you change your rules or filtering settings or correct a message afterward. When you turn off dry run, SpamWise also acts on the mail it checked during the dry run that’s still in your Inbox. If your rules and filtering settings haven’t changed since, it reuses those decisions instead of sending the messages to your provider again.

Keep SpamWise running

SpamWise checks each account’s Inbox every minute while both SpamWise and Mail are open. Closing SpamWise’s windows leaves it running in the menu bar; quitting it stops filtering. To open it automatically, turn on Open SpamWise at login in Settings → General. To check new mail the moment it arrives, see Check new mail sooner.

Sort older mail

Automatic filtering covers mail that arrives after setup, or after you include an account. To sort mail that was already in your Inbox:

  1. Turn off dry run.
  2. In Settings → Accounts, open Older mail under the account.
  3. Choose Preview older Inbox mail, then check the decisions in Activity.
  4. Choose Apply previewed decisions. To continue, increase Older mail page and preview the next page.

Previews send messages to your provider, which can cost money. Changing settings discards previews you haven’t applied.
