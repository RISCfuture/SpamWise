How decisions are made

Address rules take precedence over domain rules. Explicit rules take precedence over Contacts and known correspondence or order history. A new correction replaces a conflicting rule at the same scope. Learned signals cannot override these gates.

Optional evidence includes domain age, configured reputation services, local Rspamd analysis, and text recognized in embedded images. Missing permissions, lookup errors, unavailable services, and unknown history remain unknown; they are never proof of spam. Remote images and message links are never fetched.

Similarity retrieval selects related explicit corrections for the prompt. Campaign matching uses substantial similar text plus a sender or link-domain anchor from an explicit Junk correction. A matching Good correction invalidates learned Junk campaigns. Policy suggestions require your explicit application.

Bayes and regularized logistic estimates are weak, uncalibrated evidence and stay unavailable until enough reviewed examples exist. Held-out statistical examples never train those estimators. High-confidence predictions cannot replace explicit labels, and contradictory predictions are excluded from training.

Review stays in Inbox with an orange flag or background. Activity separates the verdict, intended action, and observed outcome. Decisions are saved before action dispatch. Errors fail open; a requested action is not proof of completion, and failed or uncertain mail actions are not automatically repeated.
