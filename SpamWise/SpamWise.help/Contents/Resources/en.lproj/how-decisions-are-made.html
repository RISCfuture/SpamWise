How decisions are made

Address rules take precedence over domain rules. Explicit rules take precedence over Contacts and known correspondence or receipt history. A correction replaces a conflicting rule at the same scope.

When no local rule or trusted relationship decides a message, SpamWise collects sender and link domains, reported authentication claims, unsubscribe presence, partial correspondence history, and optional RDAP domain age. Missing or failed evidence is unknown, never proof of spam. Arbitrary authentication headers are not trusted.

The model receives fixed instructions, your plain-English preferences, recent correction quotations, and bounded message text in separate prompt sections. Attachments and quoted reply chains are omitted. Links and images are not loaded.

Inbox means no action. Review remains in Inbox with an orange flag by default, or an orange background if selected. Junk moves to the resolved Junk mailbox of that account only when confidence meets the selected threshold and actions are enabled.

Activity separates the model verdict, intended action, and actual outcome. Dry run, paused, unclassified, integration unavailable, requested, applied, and failed are distinct states. Requested does not mean confirmed delivery. The explanation is saved before an action is requested.

Parsing, classification, storage, and integration errors leave mail unchanged. Transient classification errors can retry up to three times; corrections invalidate stale work. Failed mail actions are not automatically retried because their delivery status may be unknown.
