Reviewing Junk

  1. Choose Filtering → Review Junk, or Review Junk in the Activity toolbar.
  2. Choose an account from the Account menu.
  3. Select a message to read it. SpamWise says how it got there: SpamWise moved it, you marked it as Junk, or another filter, such as Mail’s own Junk Mail filter, or the mail server put it there.
  4. To restore a message, choose Mark as Good and Restore. SpamWise moves it to Inbox and allows its sender.

Use Previous Page and Next Page to see more of the mailbox. Nothing moves unless you choose Mark as Good and Restore.

If one of your Block rules matches the sender, SpamWise asks first. For a rule for the sender’s address, choose Replace Rule and Restore. For a rule for the sender’s domain, choose Allow Sender and Restore: the domain stays blocked, but mail from this sender is allowed.

Mail that other filters or your mail server put in Junk doesn’t teach SpamWise anything until you restore it here. To stop Mail’s own filter from moving mail, see Mail’s own Junk Mail filter.
