Using Activity

The Activity window lists every message SpamWise has checked. Open it with Show Activity in the SpamWise menu-bar menu.

Columns

  • Date: when SpamWise decided.
  • Sender and Subject.
  • Verdict: Good, Review, Junk, or Unclassified when the message couldn’t be classified.
  • Confidence: how confident the decision was.
  • Reason: why SpamWise decided as it did, such as a rule, a person you know or the provider’s explanation.

Click a column heading to sort by it. Choose Show More at the bottom to see older decisions.

Find a message

Type in the search field to search senders, subjects and reasons. To narrow the list, choose Filter in the toolbar and choose an Account, a source under Decided by, an Outcome, Corrections only, Today only or a Date range…. Choose Clear Filters to see everything again.

See the details

Select a message and choose View → Show Inspector (Option-Command-I). The inspector shows the verdict, the reason and the Outcome, which is what happened in Mail, such as Applied, Dry run, Paused or Failed. It also has the controls to correct the decision, Preview message, Open in Mail and Export raw source…. Decision details lists what decided the message, its account and mailbox, the intended action, the tokens used and the estimated cost.

Toolbar and Filtering menu

  • Pause Filtering stops sorting new mail until you choose Resume Filtering. Mail already sorted stays where it is.
  • Dry Run Recent Mail shows how SpamWise would sort your newest mail. Nothing moves.
  • Review Digest lists this week’s mail marked for Review. See the Review digest.
  • Review Junk lets you look through Junk and restore anything that isn’t junk. See Reviewing Junk.
  • Statistics, also in the View menu, shows how much mail SpamWise sorted. See Statistics.

The Filtering menu also has the correction commands and Classify Again; see Correcting mistakes. Tools → Configuration History lists changes to your rules, policy and learning.

Status

The bottom of the window and the SpamWise menu-bar menu show what SpamWise is doing, such as “Dry run — mail stays unchanged”, “Filtering paused” or “Offline — checks resume when you’re back online”. The bottom of the window also shows today’s counts and this month’s estimated cost. When something needs your attention, see Troubleshooting Mail integration.
