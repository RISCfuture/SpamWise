Notifications and the Review digest

Notifications

  1. In Settings → General, under Notifications, turn on Notify me about unresolved Review.
  2. Allow notifications from SpamWise when macOS asks.

Then choose:

  • Also include good mail, to count new Good mail too.
  • Notify at most every …, the shortest time between notifications.
  • Quiet hours, a daily period without notifications. Turn it on, then choose Quiet from and Quiet until.

Notifications start once dry run is off, and while filtering is paused they wait until it resumes. They show counts, never message subjects or senders. If Settings says notifications are denied, allow SpamWise in System Settings → Notifications.

The Review digest

Turn on Show a weekly Review digest in Settings → General. Each week, SpamWise lists the Review mail from the last 7 days that you haven’t resolved. When it’s ready, Review Digest appears in the SpamWise menu bar menu, and Open Digest appears at the bottom of the Activity window. You can open it any time with Filtering → Review Digest. Select a message to see its details and correct it. Nothing is emailed.
