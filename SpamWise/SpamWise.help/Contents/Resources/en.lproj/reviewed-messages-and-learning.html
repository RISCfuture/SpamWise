Reviewed messages and learning

Reviewed messages

Choose Tools → Reviewed Messages… to open Reviewed Messages. It lists your corrections and the messages you imported, each labeled Good, Junk or Review.

  • To add messages you’ve checked yourself, choose a label from Import as, then choose Import Messages… and select up to 20 email (.eml) files. Importing never creates rules or moves mail. Imported messages keep no message text unless Keep text of reviewed messages is on.
  • To change labels, select messages and choose Label As.
  • To remove messages, select them and choose Forget…. SpamWise also forgets what it learned from them; your sender rules stay.
  • Choose More → Export Reviewed Messages… to save the list, or More → Rebuild Learning to relearn from only the messages listed.

SpamWise keeps a limited number of examples. When an import would go over the limit, SpamWise asks before forgetting your oldest corrections as examples.

Learning from corrections

In Settings → Advanced, under Learning from corrections:

  • Recognize campaigns you marked as Junk marks Review any message that closely matches a campaign you marked as Junk.
  • Learn from words in corrected mail starts once you’ve reviewed at least 20 Good and 20 Junk messages.
  • Learn which evidence matters most starts once you’ve reviewed at least 100 messages, including 30 Good and 30 Junk.
  • Keep text of reviewed messages saves the text of up to 500 reviewed messages on this Mac, so Reviewed Messages can show them and More → Export Reviewed Messages… includes it. Turning it off deletes the kept text; labels and learning remain.

Learning only makes a verdict more or less likely. Your rules and people you know always come first, and learning never changes your filtering strength. Only messages you reviewed yourself teach SpamWise, not mail that other filters put in Junk.
