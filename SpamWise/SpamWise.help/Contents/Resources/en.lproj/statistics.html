Statistics

Choose View → Statistics, or Statistics in the Activity toolbar. Choose a period with From and Through, and an Account or All accounts.

  • Messages: how many messages SpamWise decided.
  • Waiting for Review: Review messages you haven’t resolved.
  • Mail actions that failed: moves and marks Mail didn’t confirm.
  • Decisions you reviewed and Decisions you changed: how many decisions you looked at and how many of those you corrected. “At most …% of reviewed decisions” is the largest share of decisions that, with 95% confidence, needed a change. It narrows as you review more.
  • Estimated cost: the estimated provider cost for the period, including the small test requests that check an API key.
  • Messages per day: a chart of each day’s messages, by account.

Choose Export Statistics… to save these figures as a file. Export Redacted Diagnostics… saves SpamWise’s status without keys or message content, for example to send to support.
