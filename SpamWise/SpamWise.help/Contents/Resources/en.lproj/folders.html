Folders

Besides each account’s Inbox, SpamWise can filter other folders and learn from folders you fill yourself. In Settings → Accounts, under Folders, choose Configure Folders….

Filter another folder

  1. Choose the folder from the Folder menu. If it’s missing, choose Refresh Folders.
  2. Set Purpose to Filter new mail and choose a date for Filter mail received on or after.
  3. Turn on Process this folder automatically, then choose Save Plan.

To see what SpamWise would decide first, choose Preview This Page. A preview checks 20 messages at a time and moves nothing; change Preview page to see later messages.

Teach SpamWise with training folders

  1. In Mail, create an empty folder named SpamWise Train Good or SpamWise Train Junk in the account.
  2. In Configure Folders…, choose that folder and set Purpose to Learn Good from placed mail or Learn Junk from placed mail.
  3. Choose Save Plan.

Messages you then put in the folder count as your corrections. Keep training folders separate from your Junk mailbox and from server filters.

Saved plans

Saved plans lists every folder SpamWise filters or learns from. Use a plan’s switch to stop processing the folder, or choose Remove… to delete the plan; the folder and its mail stay as they are. If you rename or delete a folder in Mail, SpamWise stops processing it and says so.
