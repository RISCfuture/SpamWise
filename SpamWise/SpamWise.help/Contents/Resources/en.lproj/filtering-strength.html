Filtering strength

Conservative requires a model confidence of at least 99 for Junk, Balanced 95, and Aggressive 90. Lower-confidence Junk verdicts become Review. These thresholds are provisional and uncalibrated. Model confidence, Bayesian log odds, logistic margins, and campaign similarity are not calibrated probabilities.

Use dry run and inspect explanations before enabling automatic actions. Conservative sends more uncertain messages to Review. Review remains in Inbox with an orange flag or background. Optional learning does not lower the selected threshold or override explicit rules and trusted relationships.

Bayes requires at least 20 explicitly reviewed training examples per binary class. Logistic fitting requires at least 100 reviewed training examples, including 30 per class. Those are availability gates, not quality guarantees. Predicted labels cannot satisfy the reviewed-data minimums.

The evaluation CLI reports observed errors, confidence buckets, costs, and candidate thresholds. With zero false positives, roughly 1,500 representative good messages are needed for a 95% rule-of-three upper bound of 0.2%. A small synthetic fixture set cannot establish this target. Choose thresholds on training data and validate them with a separate representative held-out corpus.
