Filtering strength

In Settings → General, Filtering strength sets how confident your provider must be before SpamWise moves a message to Junk:

  • Conservative: at least 99% confident.
  • Balanced: at least 95% confident.
  • Aggressive: at least 90% confident.

A Junk answer below that confidence becomes Review instead. Conservative sends more uncertain mail to Review; Aggressive moves more of it to Junk, with more risk of moving mail you want. Filtering strength doesn’t change what your rules or people you know decide.

For a kind of mail you chose Junk for in Settings → Mail Choices, filtering strength also sets how sure your provider must be that a message is that kind of mail before SpamWise moves it to Junk, unless it answered Good: 95% for Conservative, 90% for Balanced and 85% for Aggressive. Below that, or when it answered Good, the message is marked Review unless your provider answered Junk with the confidence above.

Choose a strength

Confidence is your provider’s own estimate, not a measured accuracy. Start in dry run and look at the Confidence column in Activity for messages you would call Junk. Choose the strength that moves the mail you want moved, and correct mistakes as you go: corrections create rules, which always come first.

Review mail

Review mail stays in Inbox so you can decide. Under Review treatment, choose Orange flag (keep in Inbox) or Orange background (keep in Inbox). To get a list of unresolved Review mail every week, see the Review digest.

Junk mail

To mark Junk as read once Mail confirms its move, turn on Mark as read after a confirmed Junk move.
