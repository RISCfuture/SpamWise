Filtering strength

Conservative requires model confidence of at least 99 for Junk, Balanced 95, and Aggressive 90. Lower-confidence Junk verdicts become Review. Explicit block rules are deterministic and use confidence 100.

These defaults are provisional and uncalibrated. A model’s stated confidence is not a probability. No small fixture set establishes the target of at most one good message junked in 500.

The evaluation CLI reports observed precision by confidence, false-positive bounds, and threshold candidates. With zero false positives, approximately 1,500 representative good examples are needed for a 95% rule-of-three upper bound of 0.2%. Threshold selection requires separate held-out validation.

Use dry run first and inspect explanations. Conservative reduces automatic Junk moves by sending more uncertain mail to Review. Review never leaves Inbox; choose an orange flag or orange background in General settings.
