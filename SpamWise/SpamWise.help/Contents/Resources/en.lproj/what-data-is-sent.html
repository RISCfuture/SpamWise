What data is sent

On your Mac

Your rules and known senders are checked on this Mac. SpamWise keeps its settings, rules, decisions, corrections and learning on this Mac. API keys, the Spamhaus query key and IMAP passwords are stored only in Keychain. SpamWise never reads Mail’s databases and never asks for Full Disk Access.

Your provider

With Apple on-device, mail stays on this Mac. With Claude, OpenAI or Gemini, SpamWise sends nothing until you turn on “Allow mail context to be sent to provider” under Mail sharing in Settings → Provider. After that, each message your rules and known senders can’t decide is sent to the provider with your API key. A request contains:

  • the sender, the subject and a shortened message text;
  • evidence such as authentication results and the domains of links;
  • your mail choices and additional instructions;
  • some of your similar earlier corrections, each with its sender’s domain, subject, a short excerpt and your label. They come from the same account, except reviewed messages you imported, which can be quoted for any account.

Mail can contain personal information about you and others. Choose Provider data and retention terms in Settings → Provider to read how your provider handles it. To stop sending mail, turn off mail sharing or turn on Keep filtering on this Mac. Requests already sent can’t be recalled.

Under Classification context in Settings → Advanced, Similar corrections sets how many corrections a request quotes (5 unless you change it, up to 20), and Message text sent sets how much of the message it carries (about 1,125 words unless you change it). More context can help decisions but costs more.

Dry runs and previews of older mail also send messages to your provider.

Cost and the monthly spending limit

Claude, OpenAI and Gemini charge your provider account for each message. Under Usage and spending in Settings → Provider, Month-to-date estimated API cost shows this month’s estimate.

To cap it, turn on Limit monthly spending and enter a Monthly limit. While a request runs, SpamWise counts the most it could cost; when the request finishes, its measured cost replaces that amount. When the next request would go over the limit, SpamWise stops sending mail to the provider until next month or until you raise the limit. That mail stays where it is and is classified once requests fit under the limit again.

Costs are estimates. Your provider’s bill is what you pay.

Optional checks

Each of these is off until you turn it on in Settings → Advanced:

  • Share sender domains with registration services to check domain age sends the sender’s domain to domain registration services.
  • Look up sender and link domains in Spamhaus DBL sends sender and link domains to Spamhaus. You must accept the service’s usage terms first.
  • Scan with my Rspamd server sends the whole message to an Rspamd server running on this Mac.
  • Read embedded image text with on-device OCR reads text in PNG and JPEG images inside a message; other image formats aren’t read yet. Read plain-text and HTML attachments locally reads those attachments. Both work on this Mac, but the text they find can be part of what’s sent to your provider. Remote images are never downloaded.

Keep filtering on this Mac, in Settings → Provider, blocks every provider request and network lookup. Mail still syncs your accounts as usual.

Notifications, backups and exports

Notifications show counts, never subjects or senders. The weekly Review digest stays in SpamWise and is never emailed. Backups are encrypted with your recovery key and leave out keys and passwords. Exported reviewed messages and message source can contain private mail, so keep those files private. Redacted diagnostics leave out keys and message content.
